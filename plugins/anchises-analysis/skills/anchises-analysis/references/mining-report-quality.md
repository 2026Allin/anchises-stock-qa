# Mining report quality checks

Apply these checks when the selected company is a miner, mineral explorer,
developer, or royalty/streaming company. Follow the returned sector prompt;
these checks ensure section 5 is decision-useful and internally consistent.

## Financial position and capital structure

Use the latest reported period and state currency and report date. Cover when
available:

- cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash, and short-term investments
- working capital and committed but undrawn facilities
- short- and long-term debt, convertibles, lease liabilities, maturity dates,
  interest rates, security, and covenant constraints
- net cash or net debt, with the calculation shown
- operating and investing cash use over the latest two to four quarters, the
  basis for normalized cash burn, and estimated runway
- basic shares outstanding and a reconciled fully diluted share count
- options, performance rights, restricted units, convertibles, and other
  potential equity claims
- warrants outstanding and exercisable, strike-price ranges, expiry ladder,
  in-the-money status at a clearly dated share price, potential exercise
  proceeds, and maximum dilution; show broker warrants separately
- recent placements, at-the-market programs, shelves or prospectuses, and any
  remaining financing capacity
- royalties, streams, offtake finance, prepayments, project finance, hedging,
  and material encumbrances
- committed capex, estimated remaining project funding, and the resulting
  funding gap to the next operating milestone
- market capitalization and enterprise value using a dated share price and
  stated foreign-exchange assumptions
- likely financing and dilution risk over the next 12 to 24 months

Do not double-count warrants, options, convertibles, or shares already included
in the basic count. Tie every cash, debt, and warrant figure to a source date;
write “not disclosed” when a material figure is unavailable. State formulas and
assumptions, distinguish company guidance from analyst calculation, and conclude
whether current liquidity appears sufficient to reach the next major milestone.
